Electronic Press Kit

Craig “Vinci” Jones

aka Vincithe1 · New Orleans, LA

Open full-size press photo ↗

Vincithe1 is a New Orleans rapper, designer and label founder who built his whole lane from scratch. Since dropping his first music in 2019, he's put out 16 releases, including the 13-track album The Vinci Code: Reloaded, backed by self-made visuals and a brand that runs as deep as the music.

The come-up

Vinci started Wake N Bake Clothing in 2016, putting the WNB name on tees before it was ever on a track. His eye for design had him making graphics for artists all over New Orleans, building their covers and promo, until he finally stepped to the mic for himself in 2019.

Wake N Bake Entertainment

Vinci and Gemellus built Wake N Bake Entertainment from nothing: no backing, no shortcuts, just records, visuals and a brand they pushed by hand. The label is home to Vinci's catalog, from singles like "Say I Am," "Facecard" and "Real Don" to The Vinci Code: Reloaded, with music videos shot to match the city they came from.

Long live Gemellus. Gone, but neither he nor his contributions to the Wake N Bake legacy will ever be forgotten.

Taking it west

Vinci sold his first home in 2021, and in 2024 he moved to California to grow WNB on a bigger stage. That run put him in rooms with industry powerhouses, on big stages like the Hash Cup in Los Angeles in 2025, and in the studio and on content with notable artists and influencers.

Back home, building

Back in New Orleans in early 2025, Vinci got into tech and launched VNC Labs, a group of businesses run with AI agents. He's also the author of When the Block Starts Talking.

The book · by Craig "Vinci" Jones Jr.

When the Block
Starts Talking

A novel written for young men, especially those growing up around pressure, poverty, trauma, temptation, and adult-sized expectations before adulthood ever arrives.

It's gritty on purpose, but it doesn't glorify destruction. The point isn't to make street life look exciting. It's to show the cost of bad decisions, the weight that falls on families, and the different kind of strength it takes to build a clean life when chaos is normal. The dialogue uses modern slang and the story is set in New Orleans from 2025 to 2029.
